### About the Event
​We’re thrilled to announce our next meetup, hosted in collaboration with [SAP](https://www.sap.com/)! Join us for an evening bringing together research and industry perspectives on **long-context LLMs, information retrieval, knowledge graphs, and autonomous AI agents**.

What role do knowledge graphs play as LLMs get longer context windows and AI agents move into production? Expect two technical talks, food and drinks, and plenty of time to meet researchers, engineers, students, and other members of Munich’s NLP and AI community.

#### Talks

- _**SAP (speaker TBA): Engineering Autonomous Industry Agents in Production**_

  How do we move beyond promising AI prototypes and turn autonomous agents into productive, reliable workflows? This talk explores the engineering challenges involved in bringing autonomous AI agents into real-world industry environments — the journey from experimentation to production, and the role of knowledge graphs in grounding AI systems and enabling robust real-world deployments.


      
- _**Jakob Sturm: Needle in a Knowledge Graph**_

  Do we still need retrieval when LLMs can digest increasingly large corpora through ever-growing context windows? This project investigates whether LLMs can reliably identify relevant information within large inputs by introducing a challenging benchmark based on knowledge graphs. The research examines how model performance changes as input size increases and how the ordering of information within the context affects an LLM’s ability to locate the relevant information.

  Jakob Sturm is a PhD researcher at TUM’s Social Computing Group and BMW’s Innovation & Emerging Technologies department, supervised by Georg Groh. His research focuses on Information Retrieval and Retrieval-Augmented Generation, particularly on understanding when RAG is the right approach and when other methods may be more suitable. At BMW, his industry focus is on domain-adapted RAG for quality-management processes.
